# Monthly report email (template)

Send it in the first week of each month. Keep it short: clients read the first three lines.

**Subject:** {{Business}}: {{Month}} report ({{N}} new leads)

Hi {{FirstName}},

Here's how {{Month}} went:

- **{{N}} new enquiries** from your website ({{last month: N}})
- **{{R}} new Google reviews**, now {{total}} reviews, average {{rating}}★
- Google Business Profile: **{{calls}} calls** and **{{directions}} direction requests** (from your profile insights)
- Changes made this month: {{e.g. "updated holiday hours, added 4 new job photos, published a post about boiler servicing"}}

**Next month** I'll {{one concrete action, e.g. "add a page for Round Rock to show up for searches there"}}.

Tip: {{N_review_links}} customers got a review request this month. Clicking the review link in each lead email after a job is the fastest way to grow your reviews.

Anything you'd like changed? Just reply.

{{Your name}}

---

## Where the numbers come from
| Number | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| New enquiries | Search your inbox (you're BCC'd on every lead): `subject:"New lead" to:{{owner email}} after:{{date}}` |
| Reviews | Their Google Business Profile → Reviews |
| Calls, directions | Google Business Profile → Performance |
| Search impressions/clicks (optional) | Search Console → Performance |
