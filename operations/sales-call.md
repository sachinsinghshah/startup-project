# Sales call script (15 minutes)

Have the demo open on your screen, and their current site and Google listing in other tabs.

## 1. Open (1 min)
"Thanks for taking the call, {{FirstName}}. I'll keep it to 15 minutes. Can I ask a few quick questions about how you get customers, then show you the site, and you can tell me if it's useful?"

## 2. Questions (4 min): let them talk
- "Where do most of your new customers come from today?" (word of mouth, Google, Yelp, ads)
- "When someone finds you on Google, what happens next? Do they call or fill a form?"
- "Roughly what's a typical job worth to you?" (this gives you the ROI line)
- "How many reviews do you have, and how do you get new ones?"
- "Anything you don't like about your current website?"

Write their answers down. You'll use their own words in the close.

## 3. Show the demo (4 min)
Show it **on a phone view**:
1. "One tap calls you, right here at the top and bottom."
2. "This form sends the customer's details straight to your phone and email within seconds."
3. "After a job, you click one link in that email and the customer gets a polite review request. That's how you get more 5-star reviews without asking awkwardly."
4. "It loads in under a second, which Google rewards."

## 4. Price and ROI (2 min)
"It's {{$setup}} to set up, then {{$monthly}} a month for hosting, the lead alerts, review requests, edits and keeping your Google listing up to date. You said a typical job is about {{$job}}, so **one extra job a month pays for it several times over**. There's no long contract; it's month to month."

## 5. Close (2 min)
"Want to go ahead? I'll send a one-page agreement and the setup payment link now, and you'll be live within a week."

- **Yes:** send the agreement and payment link while you're still on the call or straight after. Set their status to `onboarding`.
- **"I need to think about it":** "Totally fair. What's the main thing you'd want to think through?" Answer that. Then: "I'll keep the demo up until Friday. Shall I check in Thursday?"
- **No:** "No problem. Mind if I ask what made it a no?" Write the answer in the tracker; it's how you improve.

## Common objections
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive." | "One extra job a month covers it. If after 3 months it hasn't paid for itself, cancel; there's no contract." |
| "I get enough work from word of mouth." | "Great. Even referrals Google you before calling. This makes sure they call you and not the next result, and it builds your reviews." |
| "My nephew built my site." | "Happy to keep his design and just add the lead alerts and review system." |
| "I'm too busy." | "That's exactly why it's done for you. I need 15 minutes of your time in total: some photos and two clicks to give me access." |
| "Can I see results first?" | "That's why I built your demo before asking for anything. The setup fee covers the build; month to month after that." |
| "Do you guarantee rankings?" | "No one honest can. I guarantee the work: a fast site, a complete Google profile, and a system that gets you reviews. Those are what Google rewards." |
